FAQ

What you (probably) want to know.

Short, honest answers.

What's the difference between AIM CORE and AIM FLOW?

AIM CORE is software built around your product: 3D configurators, CAD integrations, custom plug-ins and machine apps, as a tailor-made project with a licence. AIM FLOW is your website and the work after the enquiry, for a fixed monthly fee plus a one-off setup. Together, the configurator fills in the enquiry and AIM FLOW follows it up through to the invoice.

Do you work with AutoCAD, Revit and Rhino?

Yes. We work with Autodesk AutoCAD, Revit, Inventor and Navisworks, and with Rhino, SolidWorks and Archicad. If there's no integration yet, we build a custom plug-in.

I already have 3D models. Can you work with them?

Yes. We work from your own CAD/BIM files, such as IFC from Revit or Archicad, STEP from Inventor or SolidWorks, and FBX from Navisworks. The original file stays untouched.

What does it cost?

Everything is tailor-made. AIM FLOW is a fixed monthly fee plus a one-off setup; you'll hear your price after your free AIM Scan. AIM CORE is a project with a licence; you'll hear your price after a short intake.

I already have a website. Can I still use AIM FLOW?

Yes. With Automation or an AI Employee we automate work alongside your existing site, and we can also improve your current site.

Can you work under NDA?

Yes. Part of our work, such as machine apps for industry, is done under NDA. That's why we don't show it here.

What happens to my data and my customers' data?

We work in line with the GDPR and sign a data processing agreement with every customer.
