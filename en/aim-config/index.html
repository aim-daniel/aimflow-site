Questions

What people ask.

What does it cost?

Custom-built, with a licence. You'll hear your price after a short intake.

Does it work on a phone?

Yes. Your customer turns the model with a finger and pinches to zoom.

Can it work with my web shop?

Ordering through the cart works with Shopify and WooCommerce. Taking prices and stock from your web shop is something we build as an Automation. We test it with your shop first, before it goes live.

What if we stop?

Once everything is paid, you get a copy of the configurator as it is online at that moment. You may use it, and have it hosted, for your own business with no end date, but not sell or give it away. The source code, the engine and the templates stay with AIM FLOW. Your products, prices, photos and brand stay yours. Prices and stock are no longer updated after you stop.
