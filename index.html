Frequently asked questions

What is the difference between AIM CORE and AIM FLOW?

AIM CORE is software around your product: 3D configurators, digital twins, CAD integrations, custom plug-ins and machine apps, as a custom project with a licence. AIM FLOW is your website and the work after the enquiry, for a fixed monthly fee plus a one-off setup. Together: the configurator fills in the enquiry, AIM FLOW follows it up to the invoice.

Do you work with AutoCAD, Revit and Rhino?

Yes. We work with Autodesk AutoCAD, Revit, Inventor and Navisworks, and with Rhino, SolidWorks and Archicad. If an integration doesn't exist yet, we build our own plug-in.

I already have 3D models. Can you work with them?

Yes. We work from your own CAD/BIM files, such as IFC from Revit or Archicad, STEP from Inventor or SolidWorks and FBX from Navisworks. The original file stays untouched.

What does it cost?

Everything is custom. AIM FLOW is a fixed monthly fee plus a one-off setup; you hear your price after your free AIM Scan. AIM CORE is a project with a licence; you hear your price after a short intake.

I already have a website. Can I still use AIM FLOW?

Yes. With automation or an AI employee we automate work next to your existing site, and we can improve your current site too.

Can you work under NDA?

Yes. Part of our work, such as machine apps for industry, is under NDA. That is why we don't show it here.

What happens to my data and my customers' data?

We work under the GDPR and sign a data processing agreement with every client.

What is the difference between SEO and GEO?

SEO makes Google understand your site and rank it high. GEO makes AI search engines, such as ChatGPT and Google's AI answers, understand what you do and mention your business. We do both, and SEO is in every package.

How fast will I be at the top of Google?

Nobody can promise that: Google decides the order. What we do is make sure your site is technically right and answers what your customers search for. If you want to be at the top right away, a Google Ads campaign helps.

What is a 3D configurator?

A tool on your own website where your customer puts together a product: dimensions, version and options, visible in 3D right away. You receive the enquiry with dimensions and parts list.

Does my model need to be redrawn?

No. We work from your own CAD or BIM files, such as IFC, STEP or FBX. The original file stays untouched.

How long does it take, and who owns the website?

That depends on the scope; in your AIM Scan you hear when everything goes live. What happens to your website if you stop is in black and white in your order confirmation, before you sign.
